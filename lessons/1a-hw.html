Writing

7A friend wrote in your group chat: “I need a new hobby for the winter. Any ideas?” Write a reply (120–150 words) and persuade them to try your hobby.create

Use at least 3 cleft sentences and 4 phrases from Exercise 2 of the lesson (take to it, grow on you, nothing beats, let off steam …).

Speaking

8Voice message. Record a 1-minute voice message (in Telegram or on your phone) for a friend who says “I’m too busy for hobbies.” Persuade them to try one activity. Send it to your teacher.create

🗣 Checklist before you record

  • Start with a question: Have you ever thought of taking up …?
  • Use 2+ cleft sentences — stress the key word: What I LOVE about it is …
  • Answer their objection: I know you’re busy, but what’s great is that it only takes …
  • Finish with: You should give it a go!
Self-assessment
