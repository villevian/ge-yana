🎭 Role-play with twists · 5–7 minutes

Choose a story. Yana plays herself; the teacher plays everyone else. In the middle of the conversation the teacher opens a twist card — Yana has to react. Tick the target words as you use them.

Target vocabulary (click when used)

After the role-play: Which twist was the hardest? Retell the story in 1 minute in the past (narrative tenses from 1B!).

🎲 Story cubes · Just a minute

Roll the cubes. Tell a story and use every word (click a cube when you use it). Talk for 60 seconds without hesitation, repetition or a wrong word. If you make one, the other player continues the story from that point — and gets the points.
